Our Story

Begun with the cloth, finished by hand.

We choose long-staple cotton and washed linen for how they feel after the hundredth night, not the first, and finish every hem as if it will be seen.

The result is a quiet kind of comfort: pieces that layer easily, age well and make a bedroom feel like home.
