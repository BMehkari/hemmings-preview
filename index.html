Design Versions

The logos, and eleven versions of the store. Each card says what the version is made of: its layout and style, its colours, its fonts and what is in it. Products, prices and offers are illustrative in all of them.

The LogosThe four selected logos: Enfilade, Threshold, Stitch and Thread. Each comes as a stacked logo, side-by-side logo, wordmark and icon, in colour, on dark, black and white, to download as SVG or PNG or as a zip. The page also links to all twelve concepts that were explored.Open →
