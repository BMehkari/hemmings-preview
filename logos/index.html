Hemmings

The Logos

Seven logos for Hemmings, each with its own icon and wordmark. The first four are two built on a doorway and two on the letter H with a textile feel. Number five is the arched door from store versions 2 and 3, and six and seven are two new designs drawn in the same fine-line style. Every one comes as a stacked logo, a side-by-side logo, the wordmark alone and the icon alone, in colour, for dark backgrounds, in black and in white, with downloads in SVG (for designers and printers, sharp at any size) and PNG (for everyday use, transparent background).

Colours

Three colours and nothing else, the same on every logo.

  • Sage#A5C28FAccents in the icon
  • Charcoal#2F312EThe icon and the name
  • Beige#F2ECE0The logo on dark grounds

How to use them

  • Give it room. Keep a clear margin around the logo at least as wide as the letter H in the name.
  • Mind the size. Do not show the side-by-side logo less than 120 pixels (30 mm) wide. At 32 pixels and below use the small icon alone.
  • Pick the version by background. Colour on white and light; On dark on charcoal and dark photographs; black or white when only one colour is possible.
  • Leave it as drawn. Do not stretch it, recolour it, add shadows or outlines, or retype the name in another font.
