How to use it

  • Give it room. Keep a clear margin around the logo at least as wide as the letter H in the name.
  • Mind the size. Do not show the side-by-side logo less than 120 pixels (30 mm) wide. Below 32 pixels use the small icon alone.
  • Pick the version by background. Colour on white and light; On dark on charcoal and dark photographs; black or white when only one colour is possible.
  • Leave it as drawn. Do not stretch it, recolour it, add shadows or outlines, or retype the name in another font.
