The Wordmark

The Hemmings name set in eight different typefaces, numbered 1 to 8 so you can simply say “wordmark 3”. Six have the royal, luxurious feel; two are deliberately different. Each is shown with a “by Al Karam” line on white and on charcoal, then alone on beige and on sage. Any of these can be paired with any of the icons.
